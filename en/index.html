FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what your company needs. After a conversation, you receive a proposal with a clear scope, timeline and price.

How long does it take?

It varies with the size of the project, and the timeline is set in the proposal. Since we deliver in stages, you start using the system before the project is finished.

Does my company own the system?

Yes. The system and the data belong to your company, with no per-user licenses.

Do you offer support?

Yes. After launch, the system stays in our care through monthly support plans.

Do I need to understand technology?

No. You run the business and we handle the technology, and we always explain things in plain language.
