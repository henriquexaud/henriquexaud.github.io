05Principles

What guides every technical decision.

  • Performance

    Speed is a feature.

    Slow screens cost sales and patience. We measure and optimize what users actually feel.

  • Simplicity

    The simplest thing that works.

    Nothing built “for the future”. Fewer moving parts mean lower cost and fewer defects.

  • Security

    Protection from line one.

    Least-privilege access, secrets kept out of the code and validation on every input.

  • Experience

    Made for the people who use it.

    Clear language and screens your team learns without a manual.

  • Observability

    If you can’t see it, you can’t run it.

    Logs, metrics and alerts from day one, so we act before customers notice.

  • Maintainability

    Code that lasts.

    Readable code, documented decisions and tests where they matter. The system is never held hostage.

FAQ

Before we talk.

How much does a custom system cost?

It depends on the scope, which is why we don’t have a price list. After a conversation and an assessment, you receive a proposal with fixed scope, timeline and investment. We often recommend starting with a lean first version that solves the main problem before the next stages.

How long does it take?

A useful first version usually takes from a few weeks to a few months, depending on complexity. We deliver in stages, so you start using parts of the system before the whole project is finished.

Do my company’s system and data stay with us?

Yes. The code, the data and the access belong to your company. There are no per-user fees and no vendor lock-in, and hosting costs are transparent and in the company’s name.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Yes, through support and ongoing evolution plans. If you prefer, we document everything and hand the system over to your team or to another vendor.

Do I need to understand technology?

No. You know your business and we handle the technical side. We explain decisions in plain language, and you follow the progress in the system itself.

I already use spreadsheets or an off-the-shelf system. Can we build on that?

In most cases, yes. We can integrate with what already exists, migrate the data or evolve an old system instead of starting from scratch.
